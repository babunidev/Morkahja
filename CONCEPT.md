# Morkahja — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

Morkahja is a browser-based RPG in a personally developed fantasy world. The repository's client, server and shared code indicate a persistent player experience with character avatars, encounters, inventory, achievements, expansions and social features. Its UI and encounter content are linked to original game-design and worldbuilding work.

This is a reconstruction from committed project code and README, not a complete statement of the world's canon or the historic first concept. The separate Ethera repository develops another chapter in the Morkahja world.

## Historical narrative — 2026-10-27 synthesis

The earliest dated journal entry (May 16, 2026) records organized encounter and solution material under **LudusOccursus** and **LudusSolutio**. By May 18, the repo history describes a first playable checkpoint with a browser client, API, shared contracts, avatar selection and encounter loop. The record then shifts toward presenting consequence as a world event: the May 20 event pipeline and subsequent **SCRIBES** feed gave resolved encounters, achievements and rewards a persistent narrative presence. Later progress added social identity, progression, expansions and game deployment.

This is a source-backed account of how the **implemented project took shape**, not a claim that the author's earliest creative idea began in May 2026. The foundational mythology, setting and original design motives remain Babuni.dev's creative work; where earlier history is missing, it must remain an open question.
