# 20232 — Where Two Roads Meet

**Creative:** Babuni

**Status:** Creative approval received 2026-11-27; approved specification only. Artwork pending; game activation not authorized.

## Encounter narrative

Two worn roads meet beneath the spreading branches of an old tree. Its broad roots rise from the grass, forming natural seats around the trunk.

A Grimnir craftsman sits among several neatly arranged bundles, while a Rokur traveler stands nearby, pointing toward the empty space beside him.

“I'm expecting company,” the craftsman explains.

“So am I,” replies the Rokur.

A brief silence follows.

The craftsman draws another bundle closer. A faint glow travels beneath the Rokur's ember-veined skin as she folds her arms.

Beyond them, the meadows stretch beneath a passing cloud. Somewhere among the branches, a bird begins to sing.

“Perhaps,” the Rokur murmurs, glancing along the road, “someone else can make sense of this.”

Between the two travelers lies an unoccupied stretch of grass.

## Location and environment

**Ethera's Vale — near Lovely Meadows.** An unnamed junction between two well-traveled roads, surrounded by rolling grasslands, wildflowers and scattered moss-covered stones. An old tree provides shade and natural seating among its exposed roots. Passing clouds soften the afternoon sunlight. Exact map coordinates remain unassigned.
