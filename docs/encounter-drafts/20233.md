## Encounter narrative

Beside a clear stream in the Enchanted Glades, two travelers sit upon a broad, moss-covered stone.

The Aevis speaks a name in a succession of delicate, overlapping tones. His silver hair drifts gently around his shoulders.

His Verdani companion attempts to repeat it, only to stumble over the final sound.

“That was almost beautiful,” the Aevis remarks.

“Almost?”

A quiet laugh passes between them.

The Verdani tries once more. A faint ripple moves beneath her translucent skin as the unfamiliar sounds escape her lips.

“Perhaps a shorter name would be kinder to those of us who cannot speak like the wind.”

The Aevis tilts his head, considering her words.

“Perhaps. But which part of myself should I leave out?”

For a moment, neither speaks. Between them, the stream murmurs over its bed of little stones.
