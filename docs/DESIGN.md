# Morkahja — Visual and Interaction Design

**Author:** Babuni.dev  
**Evidence:** Existing repository journal and client structure; historical report, not new visual acceptance testing.

## Player experience

Morkahja combines an illustrated fantasy encounter game with a persistent character dashboard. The original development journal records the May 2026 shift from a first playable browser checkpoint to a layered interface: avatar selection, encounter art, four solution slots, dice outcomes, rewards, a radial navigation menu and game-state overlays.

The visual language is not merely decoration. Encounter transitions deliberately pace discovery: the May 21 journal records a 720 ms encounter crossfade and 1100 ms solution/dice reveal. Reward icons animate toward footer targets and losses are visualized against avatar and outcome slots. These choices communicate consequence and progress.

## Accessibility and verification boundaries

Source presence does not prove successful operation on current mobile devices or with assistive technology. Keyboard/focus flows, reduced motion, readable contrast, touch layouts and outcome readability require their own tests. The journal's past recorded observations should not be relabeled as new results from this documentation audit.
